What Tackpic is

Tackpic is a map of photographs. A photograph is taken in the app, pinned to where you were standing, and public for 24 hours from the moment it was taken. After that it leaves everybody else's feed and map and is deleted from the server.

Your own permanent copy is not deleted. It stays in your record, and in your own iCloud if you turn that on.

Tackpic makes no claim that any photograph is true, and does not check where or when one was taken beyond what the phone reported. Nothing in the app is verified or edited by the developer.

How Tackpic limits what can be published

Some of this is enforced by the app rather than asked of you, and it is worth knowing:

  • Ten a day. Ten photographs may be public at once. A slot returns 24 hours after the photograph that spent it, and deleting one does not hand it back.

Reporting, blocking, and removal

Blocking is symmetric: neither of you will see the other's photographs anywhere in the app.
